## Evidence and copy boundaries

- Exactly 18 supplied cases across 13 suburbs; 19 suburbs have no exact local case. Every region hub has at least one case. Regional membership is an editorial grouping in service-areas.json, not proof of adjacent geography, travel times or team bases.
- The eight original suburb localContext paragraphs and eight repeated regional contexts are approved source copy, but are weak grounds for factual expansion. Do not extrapolate North Adelaide's “older homes” into heritage prevalence, apartment prevalence, construction dates, council controls, parking difficulty or guaranteed access. Do not turn Stirling's access/slope/trees prompts into claims about every hills property. Use “tell us if ... applies” where the property condition is unknown.
- No supplied evidence supports named streets, landmarks, postcode-specific housing stock, coastal corrosion rates, local seasonal gutter loads, travel times, same-day availability, a branch/office in each suburb, or suburb-specific licensing/insurance claims. Do not invent them to make pages appear unique.
- Guidance preparation frequently says “send photos”; the visible template correctly makes safe existing photos optional and accepts a description. Preserve that context. Do not request roof climbing, panel removal, heavy gate dismantling or unsafe inspection.
- Approved task distinctions above are useful background, not diagnoses, guarantees, fixed prices or completed projects. State that the on-site inspection determines cause, compatible parts and quoted scope.
- Shared general task lists and process statements can remain concise. The strongest improvement is the concrete focus explanation plus matching link labels, rather than multiplying the same broad service paragraph or swapping suburb names.
- A limited GSC baseline exists: 3 clicks and 89 property-level impressions in the requested window, including suburb page rows; see [Google CLI evidence](GOOGLE-CLI-CHECK-2026-10-10.md) for dates, aggregation and privacy limits. GA4 traffic data remains unavailable. These limited measurements do not provide sufficient evidence for noindex, redirects or consolidation; no manual audit finding here warrants them today.
