Your enquiry details

A plain explanation.

How information submitted through the MEL ONE website is used.

Enquiry form

When you submit the enquiry form, the information you provide is sent to MEL ONE by email so your request can be considered and a response can be provided. Your details are not stored in browser storage by this website.

Contact details

Your name, preferred contact method, suburb or postcode, timing and work description are used to respond to your enquiry. Please do not include sensitive financial, identity or access information in the form.

Phone and email links

These links open your chosen phone or email application. Any message you send through those applications is handled by that application and email provider.

Analytics

This website does not load third-party analytics or advertising scripts.
