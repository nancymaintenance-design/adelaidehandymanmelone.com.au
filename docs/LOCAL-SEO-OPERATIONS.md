## Consent, documentation and publication

Remove faces, number plates, house numbers, access codes and other identifying detail where needed. Keep consent and originals in controlled storage, not the public repository. Honour withdrawal requests through the publication workflow. Describe the actual suburb, task and documented result; label inspection-only records as assessment. Never infer a completed repair from a photograph alone.

For licensed or specialist work, confirm the provider and relevant documents before approval.
