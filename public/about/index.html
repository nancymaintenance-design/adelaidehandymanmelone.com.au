About MEL ONE

One place to begin with the repairs, upkeep and unfinished jobs around your Adelaide home.

For the home you live in

MEL ONE is a contact point for household-maintenance enquiries in Adelaide. The starting point is everyday life at home: a cupboard that no longer closes neatly, furniture waiting to be assembled, a worn screen or a garden that needs attention.

A single repair and a longer list both begin with the same conversation. Tell MEL ONE what needs attention and the outcome you would like, using the words that make sense to you.

Start with what you can see

Describe the job, where it is and how it affects the use of your home. Add your suburb, preferred timing and photos taken from a safe position. For assembly, a product name and the instructions help explain the task.

The next conversation covers access, materials, the proposed work and any further assessment needed. Scope, timing, availability and any quote are confirmed for each request before work is agreed. You do not need to diagnose a fault to make an enquiry.

Bring the whole household list

Several small jobs can be easier to describe together. Group your list by room or outdoor area, add a short note beside each item and point out anything that affects daily use. A loose handle, a wall patch and a flat-pack shelf can all go in the same enquiry.

A grouped list helps MEL ONE understand the request. Whether jobs can be combined is discussed after the individual tasks, access and timing are understood. If someone else approves the work, include that detail in the conversation.

A clear place for specialist work

Some household concerns need a dedicated professional, including electrical faults, structural issues and work involving formal approvals. Where a request requires a specialist, coordination options and the proposed scope are discussed case by case.

Work requiring trade authorisation must be handled by an appropriately qualified external specialist. MEL ONE is a household-maintenance contact; an enquiry does not establish a booking, professional inspection, compliance result or approval. The relevant service page explains the boundaries for that category.
