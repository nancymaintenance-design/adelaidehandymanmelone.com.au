Before you get in touch

A few helpful
answers.

All questions ↗
How can I contact MEL ONE?

Call 0416 614 281, email admin@melonemaintenance.com.au, or use the contact address 63 Pirie St Adelaide SA 5000.

What details should I include?

Describe the job, your suburb or postcode, preferred timing and the best way to contact you. If safe, include a wider photo and a closer view of the issue. Scope, availability and any quote are confirmed directly.

How are specialist requests handled?

Tell MEL ONE what needs attention. Where a request requires a specialist, coordination options and the proposed scope are discussed case by case. Work requiring trade authorisation must be handled by an appropriately qualified external specialist. An enquiry does not confirm a booking.

Can I include several household jobs in one enquiry?

Yes. Send a list grouped by room or outdoor area, with a brief description of each job. Whether jobs can be combined depends on the confirmed scope and availability.

What should be confirmed before a visit?

Discuss whether the visit is for assessment or agreed work, what is included and excluded, who can approve it, access arrangements, materials and any quote. Confirm timing and availability directly before treating a proposed visit as scheduled.

Should I buy parts or materials before enquiring?

Include the product name, instructions and details of any parts you already have. Discuss suitability and who will supply materials before buying replacements. Existing fittings, dimensions and site conditions may affect what is appropriate for the agreed task.

How should I prepare for an agreed visit?

Confirm who will provide access and mention parking, shared entrances or room-use restrictions. Keep the list, product instructions and agreed parts accessible, and plan for children and pets to stay away from the work area. Discuss heavy obstacles instead of moving them yourself.

What if the job changes after the visit starts?

Ask what the changed condition means for the proposed work, materials, timing and any quote. Additional work needs agreement before it proceeds. An item may need separate assessment, so record the next step rather than assuming it is covered by the original list.

What should I check at the end of the visit?

Compare the work discussed with your original list and ask which items remain outstanding. Clarify relevant care information and agreed waste arrangements. If a concern arises later, contact MEL ONE with the job details and safe photos where useful; further work and timing need confirmation.
