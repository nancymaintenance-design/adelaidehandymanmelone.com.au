A Room-by-Room Home-Maintenance Walk-Through

A non-invasive checklist for noticing household jobs and preparing a clear MEL ONE maintenance list.

Published 2026-09-18

Look at spaces you use every day

Walk through each room and note ordinary-use observations such as a loose handle, dragging door, scuffed surface or furniture awaiting assembly. Do not force a fitting or move a heavy item to complete the list.

Observe outside from the ground

From an accessible ground-level position, note visible concerns around gates, paths, garden beds, screens and outdoor furniture. Leave roof access, pool-barrier concerns and structural movement for appropriate assessment.

Turn observations into an enquiry

Group tasks by area and note which affect day-to-day use, access or timing. A list supports a MEL ONE conversation; scope, availability, timing and any quote are confirmed directly for the individual request.

Key notes

  • Note the room or outdoor area for each item.
  • Record visible condition and the outcome you want.
  • Observe outdoor areas from accessible ground level only.

Keep in mind

  • This is general enquiry guidance, not an inspection report or a statement that a property is safe.
  • An enquiry does not establish a booking, inspection result or final price.
