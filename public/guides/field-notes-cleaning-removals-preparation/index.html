Preparing for Cleaning, Removals and Household Clear-Out Enquiries

How to define household cleaning, item-removal and moving-preparation questions before contacting MEL ONE in Adelaide.

Published 2026-09-18

Define the area and the intended result

Name the rooms, patio, garage or accessible exterior space involved and describe the desired outcome: a cleaner area, removal discussion, moving preparation or a clear list of items to address. Separate general cleaning from household items you want to keep, donate or discuss removing.

An image or list cannot by itself confirm item weight, waste type, disposal route, access practicality or the condition of a space. A clear starting description is more useful than promising that all contents will be removed or that a specific method is appropriate.

Make a sensible inventory without handling risky material

Use a numbered list for larger items and approximate locations, plus safe photos that show pathways or access constraints. The goal is to inform a discussion, not to ask someone to lift, dismantle, move or expose a difficult item before arrangements are confirmed.

Suspected hazardous material, pest issues, mould concerns and unknown substances require appropriate specialist assessment. Do not disturb, bag, sweep, treat or transport them based on general website guidance.

Discuss access, timing and next steps directly

Mention stairs, parking, shared access, gates, children, pets and any person who needs to approve the request. A moving date or end-of-lease date can be shared as a preference or context, not as a guaranteed service time.

MEL ONE will discuss scope, access, waste type, materials, timing and any quote for the particular household request. The enquiry does not establish collection, disposal, a booking or a treatment plan.

Key notes

  • List the rooms or outdoor areas involved.
  • Separate items to keep from items to discuss for removal.
  • Note pathways, stairs, parking and shared-access limitations.
  • Photograph safely without moving heavy items or unknown material.
  • Leave suspected hazardous, pest or mould concerns untouched for appropriate assessment.

Keep in mind

  • This is general enquiry guidance, not disposal, treatment or handling instructions.
  • An enquiry does not establish collection, disposal, a booking or a treatment plan.
