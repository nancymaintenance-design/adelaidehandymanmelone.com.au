How to Prepare a Household-Maintenance Enquiry

A simple Adelaide homeowner's guide to photos, job details and questions to bring to a MEL ONE enquiry.

Published 2026-09-18

Describe what has changed

Start with the visible symptom and its location, such as a drawer catching, a screen tear or a loose fitting. Say how long you have noticed it and what outcome you want without assuming a cause.

Gather information without creating another job

Use photos from a safe position, with a wider view for context and a closer view where useful. Leave electrical fittings, roof access, broken glass and unknown materials untouched while preparing an enquiry.

Ask about the next step

Include your Adelaide suburb and preferred contact method, then ask what information or assessment may be needed. MEL ONE discusses scope, access, timing, materials and any quote for the individual request.

Key notes

  • Describe what you observe and the outcome you want.
  • Include your suburb, access details and preferred reply method.
  • Use safe photos to show context where useful.

Keep in mind

  • This is general enquiry guidance, not repair instructions or a report of customer work.
  • An enquiry does not establish a booking, inspection result or final price.
