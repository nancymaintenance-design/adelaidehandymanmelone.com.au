Pre-Sale Home Maintenance: Sorting a Practical Preparation List

A homeowner-focused way to sort visible maintenance tasks before discussing pre-sale preparation with MEL ONE in Adelaide.

Published 2026-09-18

Start with the spaces a visitor can use and see

Make a room-by-room and outdoor-area list of visible household tasks, such as a loose handle, worn screen, small surface mark, unfinished assembly or routine garden tidy-up. Grouping by location makes the list useful for a conversation without claiming every item is necessary for a sale.

A maintenance preparation list is different from building, pest, valuation or legal advice. It does not say that work will improve a sale price, satisfy a buyer, meet a disclosure obligation or replace an independent assessment.

Separate presentation tasks from questions needing assessment

Label straightforward-looking presentation tasks separately from concerns such as visible cracking, water marks, electrical fittings, roof access or wet-area issues. This prevents all tasks from being treated as equivalent when more information or specialist assessment may be needed.

A visible mark cannot confirm its cause, and a task list should not hide, cover or alter a concern simply for presentation. The next step may be a discussion, further information or an external specialist rather than a general-maintenance task.

Bring an ordered list to the enquiry

Identify your desired outcome and any timing preference, then send safe photos and a concise list with the Adelaide suburb. Mention if agent access, deliveries, other trades or family use of rooms affects how the list can be discussed.

MEL ONE can discuss the individual household-maintenance request; scope, materials, timing, availability and any quote are confirmed directly. A preparation list does not confirm a schedule, project outcome or sale-related result.

Key notes

  • List visible tasks by room and outdoor area.
  • Distinguish presentation items from concerns needing assessment.
  • Record the desired outcome, not an assumed repair method.
  • Take safe photos without covering or altering a concern.
  • Note property access and timing preferences for discussion.

Keep in mind

  • This is general maintenance-preparation guidance, not building, pest, valuation or legal advice.
  • An enquiry does not confirm a schedule, project outcome or sale-related result.
