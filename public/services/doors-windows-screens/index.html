Doors, Windows & Screens

Discuss sticking doors, worn hardware, damaged screens and window fittings around your home.

What to discuss

  • Sticking internal doors and latch alignment.
  • Door handles, hinges and lock-replacement enquiries.
  • Window catches, handles and operating hardware.
  • Flyscreen mesh, frames and screen-door enquiries.
  • Sliding-door rollers and track concerns.
  • Damaged glass and glazing enquiries.

Scope boundaries

  • Security hardware, glazing and work affecting escape routes require specialist assessment before the work method is agreed.
  • Do not handle broken glass or force a damaged door or window.
