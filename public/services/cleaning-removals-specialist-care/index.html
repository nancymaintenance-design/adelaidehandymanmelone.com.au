Cleaning, Removals & Specialist Care

Household clean-ups, unwanted furniture, end-of-lease cleaning and the heavier jobs on your moving list.

What to discuss

  • General household and end-of-lease cleaning enquiries.
  • Accessible windows, patios and outdoor-surface cleaning.
  • Unwanted furniture and household-item removal requests.
  • Small move and household rearrangement enquiries.
  • Pest and mould concerns around the home.
  • Suspected hazardous-material concerns.

Scope boundaries

  • Pest, mould and suspected hazardous-material concerns require specialist assessment; referral options are discussed for the individual request.
  • Waste type, access, item weights and disposal arrangements must be confirmed beforehand.
  • Do not disturb suspected hazardous materials or attempt treatment from general website guidance; specialist assessment is required.
