Maintenance Planning & Inspection Support

Turn a maintenance list into clear questions, supporting photos and a discussion of next steps.

What to discuss

  • Room-by-room maintenance-list preparation.
  • Pre-sale and end-of-lease work-list organisation.
  • Rental-property maintenance enquiries.
  • Photo and access information for a specialist visit.
  • Requests for property-condition or defect assessment.
  • Approval or compliance documentation questions for referral to an appropriate professional.

Scope boundaries

  • An enquiry or general maintenance list is not an inspection report, approval, compliance certificate or professional assessment.
  • Formal inspections, approvals and certifications must be obtained from the appropriate professional or authority.
