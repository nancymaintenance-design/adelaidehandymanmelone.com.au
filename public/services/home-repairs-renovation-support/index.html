Home Repairs & Renovation Support

Organise questions about a room refresh, minor repair or finishing list for an existing home.

What to discuss

  • Pre-sale touch-up lists and cosmetic repairs.
  • End-of-lease repair lists for discussion with the property owner.
  • Small-room refreshes and finishing details.
  • Surface, trim and non-structural joinery repair enquiries.
  • Tiling, flooring and wet-area concerns.
  • Visible cracks, movement and structural concerns.

Scope boundaries

  • MEL ONE is presented here as a household-maintenance contact, not a general construction contractor.
  • Structural, waterproofing and approval-dependent work needs an appropriate external specialist and a separately confirmed scope.
