Garden & Landscape Care

Discuss routine garden tasks and small improvements for your household outdoor space.

What to discuss

  • Lawn mowing, edging and general garden tidying.
  • Weeding, mulching and garden-bed refreshes.
  • Hedge trimming and small-plant pruning.
  • Green-waste collection and disposal enquiries.
  • Small paving, edging and path-maintenance concerns.
  • Irrigation systems with damaged fittings or uneven watering.

Scope boundaries

  • Irrigation-system faults may need specialist assessment; coordination options depend on the individual request.
  • Tree work, excavation and alterations near underground services need separate assessment.
  • Access, waste type and disposal arrangements must be agreed for the particular job.
