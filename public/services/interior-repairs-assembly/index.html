Interior Repairs & Assembly

Bring together the little jobs inside your home: assembly, patching, fittings and everyday repairs.

What to discuss

  • Flat-pack furniture and shelving assembly.
  • Small wall holes, plasterboard patches and surface touch-ups.
  • Curtain rods, blinds, picture hooks and household fittings.
  • Cupboard doors, hinges, handles and drawer adjustments.
  • Skirting boards, trims and minor non-structural timber repairs.
  • Household appliances with a fault or damaged fitting.

Scope boundaries

  • Fixing methods depend on the wall, load, product instructions and site conditions.
  • Appliance faults and concealed services need separate assessment; specialist referral is discussed for the individual request.
