Home Electrical Repairs

Flickering lights, worn switches, ceiling fans and power points: describe the electrical issue in your home.

What to discuss

  • Lights or switches needing professional attention.
  • Loose, damaged or non-working power points.
  • Ceiling-fan repair or replacement enquiries.
  • Household circuit faults and recurring power interruptions.
  • Home electrical fittings and replacement enquiries.
  • Air-conditioning maintenance requests.

Scope boundaries

  • Work requiring trade authorisation must be handled by an appropriately qualified external specialist. Coordination options are confirmed for the individual request.
  • This enquiry category is not an emergency response or a booking. Do not touch damaged fittings or attempt electrical repairs.
