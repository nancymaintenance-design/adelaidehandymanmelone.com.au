MEL ONE Field Notes: One List for the Jobs Around Your Home

A practical way to group household repair requests before contacting MEL ONE in Adelaide.

Published 2026-09-18

Start with the jobs you keep noticing

A cupboard door that will not sit straight, an unassembled bookshelf and a small wall mark can live on separate mental lists. Write them down together, with the room or location beside each item. This gives a clearer starting point for a household-maintenance enquiry.

For MEL ONE, a useful first message explains what you can see and what outcome you want. You do not need to diagnose a fault or select a repair method before making contact.

Give each item just enough detail

Add a brief description, an approximate size where helpful and a photo taken from somewhere safe. For assembly, include the product name and whether instructions and parts are available. For a door or fitting, explain whether the problem is constant or happens only in a particular position.

Keep personal paperwork, faces and valuables out of photos. A close view and a wider view tell different parts of the story. There is no need to open fittings, climb onto a roof or move a heavy item for a photograph.

Use the list to start a conversation

Mark anything that affects access or everyday use, then note your suburb and preferred timing. Let MEL ONE know if the property is tenanted or another person must approve the work. A grouped list helps the discussion, but does not establish what can be completed together.

Call MEL ONE on 0416 614 281 or email admin@melonemaintenance.com.au. The contact address is 63 Pirie St Adelaide SA 5000. Scope and availability are confirmed for the individual Adelaide household-maintenance request.

Key notes

  • Describe what you observe and the outcome you want.
  • Discuss scope, access, timing and any quote directly with MEL ONE.

Keep in mind

  • This is original general enquiry guidance, not repair instructions or a report of customer work.
  • An enquiry does not establish a booking, inspection result or final price.
